# Careerloom brand sheet

**Concept.** Loomi is a ball of teal yarn with a face: your career, still being wound. One amber strand has come loose and curls up like a cowlick. That loose thread is the job you haven't pulled yet. Eyes are deliberately lopsided (the right one is bigger) so Loomi looks curious, not corporate.

**Palette** (four colours, flat fills only)
- Ink `#10151d`: app-icon tile, eyes, smile, text on light surfaces
- Loom Teal `#2dd4bf`: the yarn ball, and "Career" on dark surfaces
- Deep Weft `#0f8f7e`: yarn wraps on the ball, and "Career" on light surfaces
- Amber Thread `#fbbf24` (dark) / `#d98a0b` (light): the loose thread, and "loom" in the wordmark
- Ivory `#f7f5f0`: eye glints only, plus the light background

**Wordmark.** Inter 700, tracking -0.02em. "Career" is set in teal and "loom" in amber, the same two-thread split the app uses. SVGs use live text (`Inter, 'Helvetica Neue', Arial`), so outline them before sending to print.

**Clear space.** Leave space on every side equal to the height of the curl, about 1/4 of the ball's diameter.
**Minimum size.** App icon 16px (it reads as a teal ball, dark eyes and an amber tuft). Horizontal logo 120px wide. Below that, use the mark alone.

**Do:** keep the curl amber and on the top right. Keep the eyes lopsided. Place the mark on ink, ivory or white.
**Don't:** recolour the ball, add gradients or shadows, straighten the loose thread, put text inside the app icon, or pair the mark with a second mascot.

**Decision for M2 (G1: Indian English wanted):** voice picker lists, in order, *installed macOS en_IN voices* (Aman, Rishi, Tara) as the offline Indian-English default, then Kokoro voices (British/American, labelled "not Indian-accented"), then cloud en-IN voices once a key exists (M4: Azure Neerja/Prabhat or Polly Kajal are cheaper than Cartesia; Cartesia kept as premium for latency). Runtime default = *installed Indian-English system voice* when the user's region/profile is India or they pick it; otherwise Kokoro. If Kokoro is chosen and the interview is in India, show a one-line note. Sentence-by-sentence `say` rendering (~1 s/sentence, V local n=1) means the first question is delayed ~1 s: pre-render the opening question while the session starts, and render sentence N+1 during playback of N.
